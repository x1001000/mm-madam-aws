Why does the account keep getting logged out frequently?

MacroMicro's website uses a rolling 7-day login mechanism. As long as the site is revisited within 7 days of the last login, the session will be automatically extended.

However, frequent clearing of browser cache or site data, or browsing in incognito/private mode, may remove login credentials. In such cases, the system will treat it as a logout, requiring re-login on the next visit.

To maintain a stable login session, it is recommended to:

  • Avoid manually clearing browser cache and site data
  • Use regular browsing mode instead of incognito/private mode
