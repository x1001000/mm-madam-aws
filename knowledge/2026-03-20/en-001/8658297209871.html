As a paid member, is there a limit on the number of devices I can use to log into my account?

Each account can be logged in on up to two devices simultaneously, giving you flexibility to switch between devices.
For example, logging in on both a phone and a tablet at the same time counts as two devices, even on the same network (IP).
If you're an MM Business team collaborator added under the main account, you also get the same two-device simultaneous login allowance.

⚠️ Please keep your account credentials and devices secure, and ensure they are used only by you. If any unusual login activity is detected, your account may be temporarily restricted to protect its security.
