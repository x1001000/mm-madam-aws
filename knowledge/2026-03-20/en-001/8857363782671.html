How can I unlock report content in the Blog?

MM Exclusive Reports are exclusive benefits for MM subscription members (MM PRO, MM Prime, MM Max, MM Business). Subscribing automatically grants you access to view them!

There are two main types of content:

  1. WEFC (Weekly Economic and Financial Commentary) : a weekly summary highlighting 3–6 key topics based on recent macro and market developments, with concise, data-driven insights. You can also download the PDF version.
  2. Special Topic Reports : published irregularly (about 6–8 articles per month), providing thematic analysis reports based on data, events, or market changes of major economies, helping you rationally understand important market dynamics starting from the data in real time.

Currently, there are two ways to become a subscription member:

  1. Credit Card/PayPal Subscription Choose a monthly or annual plan, with automatic renewal upon expiration for uninterrupted use. Go to the Subscription Plans introduction page, select the plan that suits you to start subscribing.
  2. M-Coin Redemption First purchase M-Coins, then use M-Coin Redemption to subscribe to the service; after the redemption period ends, you need to redeem again by yourself.
