What is the Lifetime Subscription Offer?

By continuously subscribing with a credit card, you can retain the discounted plan you joined with for life. This is our most important reward for long-term users!

MacroMicro evaluates and adjusts subscription plan prices annually based on platform development. However, as long as you continue subscribing with a credit card, you can always use the same plan at the initial discounted price, unaffected by future price increases.

⚠️ Please note: Once you cancel or interrupt your subscription, this offer will no longer be retained. When you re-subscribe, the latest price at that time will apply. Please take this into consideration.
