What if I accidentally created another account and also made a subscription there?

Accidentally double-subscribed? No problem — we can sort this out!

please don't hesitate to contact us by submitting the form  and describing your situation (the emails of both accounts, subscription dates, etc.) and our team will help you merge the accounts and sort out your subscription status.

📌 Note: MacroMicro supports multiple login methods (Email, Google, Facebook). If different emails are used, each creates a separate account. To link multiple login methods under one account, log in with your original method and go to Member Profile to add the others.

For any questions, email support@macromicro.me — we're happy to help!
