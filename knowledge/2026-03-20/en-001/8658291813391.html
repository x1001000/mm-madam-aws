Will upgrading my plan affect my current subscription period?

When you upgrade, your remaining subscription time is adjusted proportionally based on the price difference — you won't lose any time you've already paid for!

For example, upgrading from a lower-priced plan to a higher-priced plan: the system recalculates your remaining time based on the price ratio between the old and new plans and adjusts your expiry date accordingly.
