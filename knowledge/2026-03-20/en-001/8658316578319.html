Where can I cancel my subscription to the free trial?

You can cancel at any time during your trial:

Go to  Member Profile > Payment History and change your subscription status to cancelled. Please complete this at least one day before the trial expiry date to avoid being charged.

⚠️ If you don't cancel before the trial ends, the subscription will auto-renew and you'll be charged on the expiry date.
📌 Reminder: MacroMicro adjusts pricing annually. Continuous subscribers keep their original rate permanently — if you cancel and re-subscribe later, current pricing will apply.
