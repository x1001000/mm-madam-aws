How to use MM Business for team collaboration?

MM Business's team collaboration features let multiple members share download quota and charts — ideal for teams!

Setup:
Click the avatar in the upper-right corner →「Member Profile」→「Team Account Management」→ add a collaborator account to enable collaboration.

Once enabled, team members can share the data download quota (200/month on monthly billing, 500/month on annual), share chart groups and charts in「My Dashboard」, and view all members' download history.

If you run into any issues during setup, please email business@macromicro.me — we're happy to help!
