How can I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription any time by going to Member Profile > Payment History. Please note that your subscription will be auto-renewed, and the billing date is 2 days before the expiration date of your subscription plan, so please make sure to cancel your subscription at least 3 days before the subscription expiration date if you wish to do so.

 

As MacroMicro continues to grow, subscription pricing may be updated from time to time. Maintaining an active subscription via credit card or PayPal allows retention of the original plan. If the subscription is canceled or interrupted, any future subscription will follow the latest pricing.
